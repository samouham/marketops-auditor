Legal & Compliance

Our commitment to data privacy, security, and AWS Marketplace standards.

1. Terms of Service

MarketOps Cloud (MCLC) provides consulting and managed services for the AWS Marketplace. By engaging our services, you agree to comply with AWS Seller standards and maintain the integrity of your technical integrations. Our role is to facilitate the technical and commercial submission process via the AWS Marketplace Seller Portal.

2. Privacy & Data Security

We prioritize the security of your AWS environment. MarketOps Cloud does not store your AWS Root credentials. All technical work is performed via AWS IAM Role-based access (Cross-Account Roles) following the principle of least privilege. Your proprietary business data, customer lists, and financial reports are strictly confidential and are never shared with third parties.

3. AWS Marketplace Disclaimer

MarketOps Cloud is an independent consultancy. While we specialize in AWS Marketplace optimization and Technical Account Management (TAM), final listing approval and disbursement schedules rest solely with the Amazon Web Services (AWS) Marketplace review and tax teams.

4. Limitation of Liability

While we strive for 100% accuracy in all metadata and technical configurations, MarketOps Cloud is not responsible for losses resulting from AWS-side outages, policy changes, or account suspensions initiated by AWS compliance teams.
