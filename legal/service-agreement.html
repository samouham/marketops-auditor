This Master Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is a legally binding contract between **MarketOps-Cloud** ("Company") and the entity establishing a cryptographic IAM Handshake ("Client"). EFFECTIVENESS: This Agreement becomes effective upon Client’s acceptance through the MarketOps secure portal, execution of an Order Form, or the initialization of the F1-F28 Discovery Matrix.

01. Forensic Data Perimeter

1.1 Zero-PII Ingestion Mandate

MarketOps-Cloud strictly operates under a Zero-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) mandate. The Sovereign-28 Engine is architected to ignore all data stored within S3 buckets, Database rows, and application-level caches. Access is scoped exclusively to metadata telemetry required for financial forensic mapping.

1.2 Read-Only Authorization

Client grants a temporary, read-only security token via AWS STS. This handshake is cryptographically limited to CloudWatch logs, Cost Explorer APIs, and Resource Metadata. Company shall have no authority to modify infrastructure; all remediation remains under Client engineering control.

02. Success Fee Protocol

Company employs a performance-based compensation model. The "Success Fee" is a contractual obligation triggered by the identification of **Verified Recovery Potential**. Fees are calculated on the Gross Annual Recovery Potential (GARP) as defined in the **Fee Schedule (Appendix A)**.

Validation Window

Client shall have a review period of fifteen (15) business days to validate the **Forensic Artifact (cryptographically sealed via SHA-256)**. Success Fees apply to verified recovery potential mutually acknowledged or not disputed within this window.

Remittance Trigger

Fees are due upon the acceptance of the Forensic Artifact or the lapse of the review window. Remittance serves as the cryptographic unlock for documented Remediation CLI Guidance.

03. Proprietary Matrix & IP

The **F1-F28 Discovery Matrix**, the forensic findings within the Forensic Artifact, and the remediation vector generation engine are the sole Intellectual Property of MarketOps-Cloud.

Technical Delivery Specification:

"Remediation vectors are provided as documented, non-executing CLI guidance. MarketOps-Cloud does not deploy, automate, or execute changes within the Client's production environment. Final validation and execution safety is the sole responsibility of the Client engineering team."

04. Liability Limitation

05. Term & Termination

The term of this Agreement and termination rights for convenience are defined in the applicable Order Form or Statement of Work (SOW). Survival clauses apply to Section 02 and 03.
